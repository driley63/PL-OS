# Plectara social post backlog

Status: Draft for review  
Version: 0.1.6  
Created: 2026-09-12

The first batch contains 12 post concepts and 48 complete captions for Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Instagram. The working objective is brand awareness and product understanding. Each concept has a shared visual brief, on-image copy, proposed alt text, and a review note.

Weeks indicate a suggested order at three posts per week. No dates are assigned and nothing is scheduled or published. The first visual and all four of its platform captions are approved. The other 11 visuals remain briefs to produce. Copy remains a draft until reviewed.

## Working approach

- Use one 1080 × 1080 square image per concept as the initial shared design. This is a production choice for reuse, not a platform maximum. Check the final preview on all four channels.
- Facebook captions are conversational. LinkedIn adds product and design context. X versions are concise and fit the standard 280-character limit. Instagram uses short paragraphs and two relevant hashtags.
- All X captions currently contain ASCII text and no links or emoji, so their character counts match X weights. Recount if those elements are added. [X character-counting guidance](https://docs.x.com/fundamentals/counting-characters).
- Use the image itself on every channel. LinkedIn supports native image posts. [LinkedIn posting guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a522602).
- Use follow, save, and conversation prompts for this batch. The local website configuration leaves store links unset by default, and live listing availability was not verified. Add download or profile-link calls to action only after the destination is confirmed.
- Keep product posts paired with current screenshots and sample data. Finalize alt text against the finished visual. Use the approved logo geometry and jade head; license any photographs.
- Photography requirement: find real, camera-shot stock photographs. Do not use AI-generated photos, including synthetic photos offered by stock libraries. Product screenshots and simple branded graphics remain suitable for the other visual briefs.
- For each selected stock photograph, record the source link, photographer, and license. Confirm the license covers its intended organic or paid social use. Favor natural, everyday scenes with space for the headline. If photographic origin is uncertain, choose another image.
- Paid promotion is a later option. No paid budget, targeting, or publication authorization is included in this backlog.

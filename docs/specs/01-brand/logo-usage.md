# Logo Usage

Status: Approved for PL-OS v2.0.0
Owner: Brand Working Group

## Sources and spacing

Use `assets/brand/plectara/source/` for current SVGs and `assets/brand/plectara/png/` for generated PNGs. Never recreate the wordmark with a font or redraw the symbol from a screenshot.

Allow one head diameter of clear space around the standalone symbol and half a head diameter around a lockup. Keep photography, badges, and dense interface content outside that space.

## Sizes and backgrounds

| Context | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Full woven symbol | 32 px or larger preferred |
| Favicon | 16 px minimum; silhouette only at that size |
| Horizontal digital lockup | 140 px minimum width |
| Horizontal print lockup | 35 mm minimum width; proof the actual output |
| App icon master | Opaque 1024 × 1024 square |

Use the full-color/ink-lettered mark on ivory or white. Use the reversed version on ink or another approved dark background. Ink and white monochrome variants are available for one-color contexts. Verify edge visibility on any other background.

## Misuse

Do not stretch, rotate, crop, add effects, recolor individual strands, alter the six-unit gaps, replace the lettering, or reintroduce the legacy gradient. Do not use AI Purple as a generic logo color.

The optional supporting line “A healthier whole” is campaign copy, not part of the master logo. Do not add legal suffixes or registration symbols unless separately authorized and substantiated.

See the [brand kit](plectara-brand-kit.md) and [construction rules](logo-construction.md).

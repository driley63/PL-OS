# Logo misuse

Do not stretch the mark, redraw the wordmark, change individual strand colors, widen or narrow its separation channels, add gradients/shadows, or use the old leaf icon.

Use approved light, reversed, and monochrome exports instead. See [logo usage](../logo-usage.md).

In a full-color logo, an ink, white, or teal head is incorrect: the head is always jade `#76B7A5`. A jade head combined with otherwise monochrome artwork is also incorrect. Monochrome means the entire figure and lettering share one ink or white fill.

Use ink lettering on light backgrounds and white lettering on dark backgrounds. Do not turn the lettering jade or recreate it by typing “Plectara.” [Use the current samples and downloads](../plectara-brand-kit.md).

# Logo System

Status: Owner-approved artwork
Version: 2.2.0
Color standard approval: 2026-09-07

Plectara's approved mark is a woven human figure with teal, jade, copper, and slate strands, a circular head, and the restored outlined wordmark. The owner approved the final lettering and consistent strand spacing.

**Always use jade (`#76B7A5`) for the head in every full-color logo**, on both light and dark backgrounds. The full-color figure does not change with the theme. Ink-headed color versions are superseded. For monochrome use, the head, every strand, and the lettering all take the same ink or white fill.

## Variants

| Variant | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Full-color horizontal | Jade head and ink lettering on white or ivory |
| Reversed horizontal | Same jade-headed color figure; white lettering on dark backgrounds |
| Ink monochrome | Entire figure and lettering in ink `#192D38` on light backgrounds |
| White monochrome | Entire figure and lettering in white `#FFFFFF` on dark backgrounds |
| Stacked | Jade-headed color figure and ink lettering for portrait placements on light backgrounds |
| Standalone symbol | Jade-headed full color, all-ink, or all-white; no lettering |
| Opaque square app icon | Platform-masked launcher and store exports |
| Native Liquid Glass iOS icon | Editable layers with default, dark, clear, and tinted system appearances |
| Rounded avatar | Social profiles and informal brand tiles |

The canonical artwork and all derivatives are in `assets/brand/plectara/`. See the [brand kit](plectara-brand-kit.md) for downloads. The PL-OS documentation system uses the Plectara identity rather than introducing a separate product logo.

The native iOS icon is an owner-approved platform treatment (2026-09-11). Its vector geometry and full-color palette are unchanged. Apple supplies material effects and may render the whole figure monochromatically in clear or tinted modes. Keep the flat masters for all other logo placements.

## Lettering

Use only the approved outlined Plectara lettering: ink on light backgrounds, white on dark backgrounds. Keep the supplied shapes, capitalization, spacing, and alignment. Inter is for UI and supporting copy; it is not a replacement for the logo lettering. See the [lettering samples and downloads](plectara-brand-kit.md#lettering-standards).

The logo identifies the product, not AI authorship. AI badges must still follow the dedicated AI visual language. Legacy LifestyleIQ and Patternara explorations are historical assets, not approved alternatives.

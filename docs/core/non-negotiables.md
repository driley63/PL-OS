# Non-Negotiables

Status: Released
Owner: PL-OS Maintainers
Version: 1.0.0
Last updated: 2026-08-02

## Purpose

Defines stable boundaries that require extraordinary review to change.

## Scope

- Medical claims
- AI behavior
- User data
- Privacy
- Accessibility
- Visual consistency

## Requirements

- Plectara must not present itself as a diagnostic device.
- AI must not replace professional medical advice.
- Users must control their personal health data.

## Implementation Guidance

- Treat non-negotiables as constraints during product ideation.
- Escalate any change that weakens them.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A proposal can be rejected by citing a non-negotiable.
- Safety and trust remain visible in review.
